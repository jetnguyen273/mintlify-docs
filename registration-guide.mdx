[**PHS OpenAPI Registration Form**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1oeHSae-jJ7wZpBbkkIYOUjHnBA98VQeV/view?usp=drive_link).

To request access to **PHS OpenAPI**, please:

1. **Download** the [**PHS OpenAPI Registration Form**](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1oeHSae-jJ7wZpBbkkIYOUjHnBA98VQeV/view?usp=drive_link).
2. **Complete** all required information and sign the form (company stamp if applicable).
3. **Submit** the completed form to [**support@phs.vn**](mailto:support@phs.vn) for review.

Once your application has been received, PHS will review your request and respond as soon as possible.

By submitting the application, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to comply with:

- PHS OpenAPI Terms & Conditions
- Risk Disclosure Statement
- API Documentation and Technical Guides
- All applicable policies, operational requirements, and technical standards issued or updated by PHS from time to time

> **Note:** Access to PHS OpenAPI will only be granted after your application has been reviewed and approved by PHS.
