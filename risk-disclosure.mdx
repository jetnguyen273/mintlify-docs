Using **PHS OpenAPI** involves risks associated with electronic trading, system connectivity, and data processing. Customers are encouraged to carefully review this document before activating and using the service.

## Client acknowledgement

Before activating PHS OpenAPI, please confirm that you have:

- Read and understood the **PHS OpenAPI Terms & Conditions**.
- Read and understood this **Risk Disclosure Statement**.
- Reviewed the **API Documentation** and related technical guides.
- Understood the capabilities, limitations, and intended use of the API.
- Acknowledged that API requests made using your credentials (API Key/Access Token) are considered authorized by you or your approved application.
- Implemented appropriate measures to protect your API credentials and authentication information.
- Agreed to comply with all applicable PHS policies, technical requirements, and guidelines as updated from time to time.

<Note>
  This Risk Disclosure Statement is provided to help customers understand the risks associated with using PHS OpenAPI. It is intended to supplement, and does not replace, the **PHS OpenAPI Terms & Conditions**.
</Note>
